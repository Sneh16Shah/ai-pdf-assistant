Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2, 2026

Introduction

AskMyPDF ("we," "our," or "us") is an AI-powered PDF chat assistant that helps users understand and interact with PDF documents. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our Chrome extension and web application.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

Information We Collect

Data Collection Summary

Data Type Collected Purpose
Email address Yes Account creation and authentication
Password Yes Stored as a secure hash — never in plaintext
PDF content Yes Temporarily processed for AI analysis
Chat messages Yes Stored to provide chat history
Browsing history No —
Location data No —
Financial information No —

How We Use Your Information

  • Authentication: Your email and password are used solely to create and secure your account.
  • PDF Processing: When you upload a PDF, its text content is extracted and processed by our AI service (Google Gemini) to answer your questions. PDF files are stored temporarily on our servers for session continuity.
  • Chat History: Your questions and AI responses are stored so you can resume previous sessions and review past conversations.
  • Service Improvement: We may use aggregated, anonymized usage data to improve the service.

Data Storage and Security

  • Passwords are hashed using industry-standard algorithms (bcrypt) and never stored in plaintext.
  • Authentication uses JWT (JSON Web Tokens) with secure signing.
  • All data is transmitted over HTTPS in production.
  • PDF files are stored on our servers only for the duration needed to maintain your session.
  • You can delete your sessions and associated data at any time from the dashboard.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to provide our functionality:

  • Google Gemini AI: PDF content and your questions are sent to Google's Gemini API to generate responses. Google's privacy policy applies to their processing of this data.
  • Render.com: Our backend and frontend are hosted on Render. Their infrastructure security policies apply.
  • PostgreSQL (Supabase/Render): User accounts, sessions, and chat history are stored in a PostgreSQL database.

We do not share your data with any other third parties, advertisers, or data brokers.

Chrome Extension Permissions

Our Chrome extension requests the following permissions, each for a specific purpose:

  • activeTab: To detect PDF files in your current browser tab.
  • storage: To store your login token locally in the browser.
  • scripting: To inject the PDF detection script that shows the "Ask AI" button.
  • sidePanel: To display the AI chat panel alongside your PDF.
  • contextMenus: To add a right-click "Ask AI" option for selected text.
  • tabs: To detect when you navigate to a PDF document.
  • Host permissions (all URLs): PDFs can be hosted on any website, so the extension needs access to all URLs to detect and fetch PDF files for processing.

Data Retention

  • Chat sessions are retained until you delete them from your dashboard.
  • Uploaded PDF files are retained on our servers for session continuity and are periodically cleaned up.
  • You can delete your account and all associated data by contacting us.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data stored in our service.
  • Delete your sessions and chat history at any time.
  • Request deletion of your account and all associated data.
  • Opt out of the service by uninstalling the extension and deleting your account.

Children's Privacy

AskMyPDF is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
